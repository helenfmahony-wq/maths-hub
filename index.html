How student practice works

Scaffolding that fades as skills grow

Once a concept has been introduced in class, students move into practice on their own — starting with the same tool available to support them, fading out as they build independence.

Already happened, in class

Teacher introduces the concept live using a digital manipulative (e.g. a double number line).

Then, students practise on their own
Level 1 · Guided

Tool available

Students answer questions similar to the worksheet with full access to the same manipulative the teacher used, plus instant marking.

Middle levels

Support fades

The manipulative is progressively withdrawn across as many levels as the question set needs, while instant feedback continues throughout.

Final level · Independent

No tool

Just the question and instant marking — a genuine check of independent understanding.

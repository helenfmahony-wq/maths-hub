Chance Lab

The computer does the experiment. You record every result on your worksheet.

  1. Choose the experiment that matches your worksheet.
  2. Press the big button once. Write the result in the next box on your worksheet.
  3. Repeat until you have done at least 30 trials.
  4. Check your worksheet against the results list at the bottom.

Think about it
  • After a cooked egg is smashed, does the chance of a raw egg go up or down? Why?
  • After a raw egg is smashed, what happens to the chance? Why?
  • Is it better to go first or second? Use the "Who is likely to lose" bar and the "Play it many times" section to decide.
  • When could the chance of a raw egg be 0? When could it be 1?
  • Run 10 games, then 1000 games. Which results are closer to the theoretical bar? Why?
  • Change the number of raw eggs. How does that change who is likely to lose?

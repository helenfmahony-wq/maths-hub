How it works

Every topic follows the same levels

Your teacher shows you the idea in class first. Then, on this page, you work through it yourself.

Already happened, in class

Your teacher introduced the idea using a digital manipulative (e.g. a double number line).

Then, you practise on your own
Level 1 · Guided

Tool available

You get the same manipulative your teacher used, and instant feedback on every answer.

Middle levels

Support fades

The tool becomes less available as you move up — feedback stays instant the whole way.

Final level · Independent

No tool

Just you and the question — a real test of what you've learned.
